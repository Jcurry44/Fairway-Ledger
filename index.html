Scorecard flow

Choose how the Card scorecard arranges each hole's inputs. "Default" leads with Score and Putts (outcome-first). "Narrative" mirrors how you played the hole — tee shot first, then approach, then green, with the final score at the bottom. Switching is non-destructive — any data you've already entered stays intact.

You

Your Bag

Tap clubs to add or remove them from your bag. Only clubs in your bag appear as options when you log a round. Old rounds that used a club you've since removed still keep it.

Safety net

Backups

The app automatically saves a snapshot before any change that could lose data (Clear, Sample Data, Import) and roughly every 10 minutes while you're actively editing. Keeping the last 20 snapshots. Tap Restore on any row to roll the entire app back to that point — the current state is snapshotted first, so a restore can itself be undone. Every 5 new rounds, the app also auto-downloads a backup file (fairway-ledger-YYYY-MM-DD-auto.json) to your Downloads folder for true off-device safety. You can also Export on demand from the header.

    Danger zone

    Advanced data controls

    These actions can permanently destroy your rounds. The app always takes a snapshot first (see Backups above), but snapshots live in this browser too — anything that wipes browser storage takes them with it. Export to a file first if you want a copy outside the browser.
